If you spend 6+ hours daily typing or using a mouse, wrist strain is inevitable—unless you invest in proper support. A quality wrist rest isn't just a luxury; it's a preventative tool against repetitive strain injury (RSI), carpal tunnel syndrome, and general neck and shoulder pain. The right wrist rest positions your wrists in a neutral alignment, reduces tension on tendons, and makes your workday genuinely more comfortable.

We've spent weeks researching, testing, and comparing wrist rests across different price points and materials. Whether you're working with a mechanical keyboard, a low-profile laptop keyboard, or a gaming mouse that demands precision, we've found options that deliver real results. This guide covers the best wrist rests currently available, breaking down specs, use cases, and honest assessments so you can make an informed choice for your specific setup.
