## Key Factors We Evaluated

When selecting surge protectors for this guide, we considered several critical factors. **Joule rating** tells you how much energy the protector can absorb before failing—higher is better, with 2,000+ joules being appropriate for sensitive electronics. **Clamping voltage** (ideally under 400V) indicates how quickly the protector responds to spikes. **Warranty coverage** varies significantly; premium models often include connected equipment protection of $50,000–$300,000. **Outlet spacing** matters more than you'd think—too close together and you'll struggle to fit multiple plugs. Finally, **brand reputation** and customer reviews give real-world insights into reliability beyond spec sheets.
