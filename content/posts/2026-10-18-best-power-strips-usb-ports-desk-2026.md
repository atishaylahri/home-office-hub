## Final Verdict

The best power strip for your desk depends entirely on your specific setup and priorities. For most people, we recommend the **Belkin SurgeStrip** as the ideal balance of reliability, outlet count, and price. It's been tested by millions of users, includes solid surge protection, and won't feel like overkill for typical home office setups.

However, if you have USB-C devices and want future-proof charging, the **Anker PowerStrip** with its 20W USB-C port offers better long-term value. If space is your constraint, the **Tripp Lite TravelStrip** is genuinely excellent for minimalist desks. And if you want smart home integration and energy monitoring, the **CyberPower CSP604S** justifies its higher price.

Regardless of which model you choose, prioritize surge protection above all else—your expensive electronics depend on it. Look for at least 1000 joules and individual outlet protection. Avoid cheap power strips that claim to offer USB charging but deliver minimal amps; they charge devices slowly and create frustration. The models above all offer reliable protection and reasonable charging speeds, so pick the one that fits your desk layout and device ecosystem, then enjoy the freedom from cable clutter.
